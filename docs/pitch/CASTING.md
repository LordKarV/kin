# Casting scaffold for the launch film

Everyone on camera is a real person who uses the app on a real call. Nobody is paid or costumed, and nobody plays a
bot or a synthetic voice. People can appear under their own name or under the story name from
docs/collab/STORIES.md, whichever they prefer (see "Names" below). Their lines are prompts. They say them in their
own words, but they keep the key nouns (Lin, the climbing wall, Moganshan, Ravi, Adyar, Kovai), because those are what
KIN has to catch.

What we need, in order of how hard it is to find:

| Order | Role | Hard requirement | Time needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paatti | Native Tamil speaker, an elder, with a family member present | 45 min including setup, about 30 s in the cut |
| 2 | Mei | Native Mandarin speaker, also speaks English | 60 min across two calls, about 35 s in the cut |
| 3 | Arjun | Teammate, steady on camera | All shoot blocks, about 70% of the cut |
| 4 | Priya | Any English speaker | 10 min, about 5 s in the cut |
| 5 | Narrator (optional) | Clear, unhurried voice | 30 min recording |
| 6 | Native checkers (off camera) | One Mandarin and one Tamil reader | 15 min each |

## Names

Option A (preferred if the cast agrees): people use their real first names in the app, and the story bends around
them. KIN builds the graph from the calls, so the cards will read with their real names. Update the narration to
match.

Option B: people use the story names (Arjun, Mei, Priya, Kamala Paatti). Then the end card says "Real people on real
calls through KIN. Names and the six-week gap were set up for this demo." Do not caption a real city, school or
relationship that is not true for that person. If the person playing Paatti is not someone's grandmother, she is
labelled what she is ("Arjun's aunt", "a family friend").

---

## Arjun (the user)

- **Who:** a college student in Austin who lives far from a high school friend and a grandmother.
- **On camera:** the lead in every call. Holds his phone for over-the-shoulder shots, reacts to cards in a small,
  natural way, says his own news back.
- **Language:** English. Can say a few Tamil words to Paatti if he knows them; not required.
- **Lines (prompts):**
  - To Priya, last week: "Oh, I started bouldering at the campus gym." (meaning: his own news, told to one friend)
  - To Mei, call 2, after the chip appears: "Oh, I actually started bouldering!" (He is sharing news, not
    remembering something he forgot.)
  - To Paatti, after his chip: "Mei and I went hiking when she visited."
- **Time:** every shoot block (about 4 hours total). Fully on camera.
- **Remote:** no, he is the anchor in the Austin location.
- **Who could play it:** a teammate. Ideally the presenter, so the film and the live pitch share a face.
- **Fallback:** any teammate. Rename the account and update the VO.

## Mei (friend abroad, Mandarin)

- **Who:** Arjun's high school friend, studying in Shanghai, speaks Mandarin and English and switches between them.
- **On camera:** two calls with Arjun. Face in picture-in-picture, her phone screen recorded. In call 2 her screen
  must show captions only, which proves the Lin card is private to Arjun.
- **Lines (prompts):**
  - Call 1 ("September"): "我最近加入了复旦的攀岩墙，认识了一个新朋友，叫林。" (I recently joined the Fudan climbing
    wall and made a new friend called Lin.) Then a little small talk in either language.
  - Call 2 ("six weeks later"): "我和林终于去了莫干山！" (Lin and I finally did the Moganshan trip!) Then: "我们爬了一整天。"
    (We climbed all day.)
  - Reaction when Arjun says he started bouldering: anything natural, in either language.
  - Next-day take: the same Moganshan line again.
- **Time:** about 60 minutes (rehearsal 15, call 1 15, call 2 plus retakes 25, next-day take 5). On camera.
- **Remote:** yes if she has an iPhone with the KIN build installed (TestFlight, see "Remote cast" below). A real
  remote call is the best version of this shot. Shanghai is 13 hours ahead of Austin, so 8-10pm CDT is 9-11am in
  Shanghai.
- **Who could play it:** a Mandarin-speaking friend of the team, a friend actually studying in China or Taiwan, a
  Mandarin-speaking hacker at HackTX.
- **Where else:** UT Austin Chinese Students and Scholars Association (CSSA), Taiwanese Student Association, the
  HackTX Discord (https://discord.gg/wBXPJAMfsP), other teams at the venue.
- **Fallback:** film her in person in a different room at the venue on a team iPhone. Do not caption her location as
  Shanghai unless that is true. If Mandarin ASR is unreliable on the day, she code-switches more English; the Lin line
  stays in Mandarin.

## Kamala Paatti (grandmother, Tamil)

- **Who:** Arjun's grandmother in Madurai. Talks with him as an equal, not as a story archive.
- **On camera:** one short video call. Her phone shows Tamil captions of Arjun's English and, at the end, a Tamil card
  explaining who Mei is.
- **Lines (prompts):**
  - "ரவி நேத்து அடையாறுலேர்ந்து போன் பண்ணான்." (Ravi called yesterday from Adyar.) This is Ravi mama, Mom's
    younger brother.
  - "ரவி கோவையில புது கடை திறந்திருக்காரு." (Ravi opened a new shop in Kovai.) This is Ravi periyappa, Dad's elder
    brother; the respectful ending is part of the clue.
  - "உன் friends எப்படி இருக்காங்க?" (How are your friends?)
- **Time:** about 45 minutes including setup and a practice run with the family member, 5-10 minutes of it on camera.
- **Remote:** yes, ideally. India is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of Austin, so 9:00pm CDT is 7:30am IST.
- **Who could play it:** a teammate's or friend's grandmother or great-aunt, in India or in Texas.
- **Where else:** UT Austin Tamil Students Association, family friends in Austin or Houston, a Tamil-speaking parent
  of a friend.
- **Must have:** a family member next to her who explains the video in Tamil, gets her consent, and holds or props up
  the phone.
- **Fallbacks, in order:**
  1. Audio-only call with the family member holding the phone. Her captions and card are still recorded from the
     phone screen; her face is optional.
  2. A Tamil-speaking adult (aunt, family friend), labelled as who she really is. Never a young person in costume.
  3. Cut her Tamil card, keep the two Ravis captions on Arjun's side only.

## Priya (friend at another college)

- **Who:** a friend from the same high school group, now at another college.
- **On camera:** 5 seconds. Arjun tells her he started bouldering. This is what tells KIN that Priya has heard the
  news and Mei has not.
- **Language and lines:** English. "No way, since when?" or anything natural.
- **Time:** 10 minutes. Can be audio-only or face-off-camera. Can be remote on any iPhone with the build.
- **Who could play it:** any friend of the team.
- **Fallback:** Arjun texts KIN the news instead (if the SMS route works), or the fact is planted with
  `POST /api/dev/apply` and the Priya shot is cut. The narration changes to match what was actually filmed.

## Narrator (optional)

- **Who:** the teammate with the calmest voice. Use the presenter if possible.
- **On camera:** no, voice only. Records the VO in a closet or a car with the engine off (soft surfaces kill echo).
- **Time:** 30 minutes, recorded last so it matches the footage.
- **Fallback:** the Arjun teammate. If no one is happy with their VO, use on-screen text only.

## Native checkers (off camera)

- One Mandarin reader and one Tamil reader check every caption and card that appears in the cut before export.
- 15 minutes each, can be done over a shared screen.
- Mei and Paatti's family member can double as checkers.
- A wrong caption is not fixed in the edit. It is either retaken or left out (see LAUNCH_VIDEO.md, retake plan).

## Remote cast: the app has to be on their phone

A real remote call needs the KIN iOS build on the remote person's iPhone. Options:

1. TestFlight internal testing (needs the team's paid Apple Developer account; the tester must be added in App Store
   Connect). VERIFY tonight that the account exists and that internal builds skip review.
2. If that is not possible, film the person in person on a team iPhone and follow the Option B labelling rules.

## Message to send (copy and edit)

> lives. Could you do two short video calls with [Arjun] tonight, around [time]? You'd speak Mandarin like you
> normally would, a couple of lines we'll send you (in your own words is fine). About an hour total. The calls go
> into a public demo video on YouTube, so your face and voice would be in it. Totally fine to say no.

To Paatti's family member:

> We're making a short demo video for a hackathon app called KIN that captions calls between grandparents and

> chats in Tamil; we'll send three lines she can say in her own way. It would be in a public YouTube video. Could you
> sit with her, explain it in Tamil, and help with the phone? Happy to call first and walk you through it.

To Priya:

> Can you jump on a 5 minute call with [Arjun] tonight for our HackTX demo video? Just react to his news. Voice only
> is fine. It goes in a public YouTube video.

## Consent and release

Get it in writing before filming (a text reply or a short form). For Paatti, the family member reads it to her in
Tamil and replies on her behalf only after she agrees.

> I agree to be filmed for the KIN HackTX 2026 demo video and to my calls being processed by KIN (my speech is
> transcribed and translated; audio is not kept; transcripts and facts are stored on the team's server and deleted
> after the event on request). I understand my face and voice will appear in a public YouTube video that stays online
> after the event. I can withdraw before the video is uploaded. Name, date.

Rules while filming:
- Show each person the "KIN is on" banner and the pause button before the call starts.
- No real phone numbers, addresses, health details or third-party names the cast did not agree to.
- After the shoot, delete the cast's worlds with `DELETE /api/me` (or the delete screen) once the cut is locked, and
  tell them it is done.
