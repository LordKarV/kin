## Rules that affect us

- "A video demoing your project must be submitted to this DevPost by the submission deadline." No length or format is
  published. Our target is 2:20, cap 3:00. (Devpost overview)
- "If your repo and video are not public, new winners may be selected." Both stay public after the event. (MLH rules,
  hacktx26.devpost.com/rules)
- "Your code must be available publicly" and "must remain public post event to be eligible for prizes." (MLH rules)
- "All projects must be built during the hacking period." Ideas may predate the event; code and materials may not be
  reused. "Teams must stop hacking once the time is up" (small bug fixes only). hacktx.com FAQ: "All work must be done
  at the event." The repo has commits dated before Oct 24 (d58c76d, 920d41b, ec0ef84 and others); prepare an exact
  answer.
- "Teams should be honest and transparent about the AI code tools they used," including listing them in the
  submission. We list Claude Code. (MLH rules)
- Team members "must be present in person during judging to be considered!" (Requirements)
- Team size 1-4. Eligibility: 18+, college students, US only. Bring a university ID and an ID proving 18+.
- Judges weigh Technology, Design, Completion and Learning equally, and "are free to make decisions based on their gut
  feeling". (Devpost, MLH rules)
